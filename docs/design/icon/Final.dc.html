MONOCHROME (ANDROID 13 THEMED) & NOTIFICATION
The gap survives flattening to one colour because it is a cut in the letter, not a second tone — the failure mode that would have killed directions A and C.
WHY THE PATH, NOT THE FONT
A shipped icon must not depend on a font being present or on a renderer's hinting. The glyph is converted to outlines once, here, and the path is the asset from then on.
WHY TWO CLIP GROUPS
VectorDrawable has no mask element, so the gap cannot be a knockout rect. The letter is drawn twice — once clipped above the gap, once below — which is exactly equivalent and needs no runtime support.

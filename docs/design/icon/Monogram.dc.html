Monogram — six executions
The direction is settled: a letter on the primary fill. What was ugly about the first pass was the execution — a default Inter Tight R, optically low, filling the whole safe zone, its counter fighting the rule beneath it. These six vary the letterform, the crop and the idea. Same teal, same flat treatment, same 108dp canvas throughout.
D1
Corrected
circle · squircle · 24dp
The same idea, drawn properly: optically centred rather than nudged, and sized to sit inside the safe zone with air around it instead of filling it. No rule underneath.
COSTNil — one glyph.
RISKStill the most common icon shape there is. Correct, not distinctive.
D2
Bookish serif
circle · squircle · 24dp
A high-contrast serif with a real spur and bracket. This is a reading product; a bookish letterform says so where a UI sans cannot.
COSTOne extra face — icon only, converted to a path. It never ships in the app.
RISKDeliberately breaks the one-family rule. Defensible for a mark, but it is a departure you should agree to.
D3
Cropped
circle · squircle · 24dp
Oversized and bled off two edges, so the icon is a fragment of a letter rather than a letter in a box. The bowl and leg become abstract shapes.
COSTNil, but the crop must be re-checked against every mask shape.
RISKThe circle mask eats more of the leg than the squircle does. Confident when it works; illegible if the crop drifts.
D4
Recall gap
circle · squircle · 24dp
The R cut by a horizontal gap — the letter is partly forgotten, and your eye completes it. That completion is exactly what the product asks you to do every morning.
COSTLow. One glyph, one knockout rect.
RISKAt 24dp the gap is under a pixel and may fill in or look like a rendering fault. Needs a second, wider-gap version for small sizes.
D5
Cloze R
circle · squircle · 24dp
The R sitting in a cloze blank — the wash fill and 2dp underline from ClozeText.kt, exactly as the app draws it. Monogram and product idea in one mark.
COSTLow, and it reuses tokens that already exist.
RISKLight ground — it can vanish on a pale wallpaper, and it is busier than the others at 24dp.
D6
Knockout
circle · squircle · 24dp
Letter punched out of a teal disc on near-black — the dark theme's ground and lifted primary. The R is wallpaper showing through, not ink.
COSTLow. One mask.
RISKA dark icon is a commitment — it looks deliberate in a dark dock and heavy in a light one.
WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG WITH THE FIRST ONE
Three things, all fixable: the glyph was nudged with a hard pixel offset instead of optically centred; it filled the safe zone edge to edge, so it read as cramped; and the ghosted rule beneath it competed with the letter's own counter. D1 is that same idea with those three fixed — worth comparing against, even if you end up elsewhere.
MIXABLE
These are not six sealed options — the axes combine. The serif letterform (D2) can take the recall gap (D4); the knockout (D6) works with any of the letterforms; the crop (D3) is independent of typeface. Say which parts you like and I will draw the combination.
